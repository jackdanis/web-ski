    How this was made: run lines and names are from OpenStreetMap via Overture Maps, and elevation is the USGS 10 m DEM. Only Max Park and Powderhorn have the 2 m detailed surface so far; the other seven are timed on the 10 m mountain, so their times will move once they get detail. The test skier steers along the route, tucks on straights, brakes only for turns tighter than it can hold, and poles below 25 km/h. Steepest is the biggest drop over any 50 m stretch of the route. All times are estimates for sign-off, not final medal targets. The Physics Lab's carve proposal will change them too.
